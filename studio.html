03 History

Two products first.
Then a studio.

Nexora did not begin as an agency. It began with products the founders had to design, ship and run themselves.

  1. OriginThe first product

    Arise

    The three founders built Arise, an AI-guided admissions platform for students in Uzbekistan. A multilingual mentor, a personal roadmap and a mobile product measured in production — not in a pitch deck.

  2. ThenThe second platform

    DigitalLib

    DigitalLib followed: a national reading platform with 713 books, a private academic vault and a 7 MB Android build made for the connections people actually have.

  3. 2026The studio

    Nexora Studio

    The collaboration became a studio. Nexora now applies the same standard — strategy, design, engineering and operations under one roof — to a deliberately small number of client engagements each quarter.

  4. TodayTwo tracks, one team

    Products and client platforms

    In-house products and client work share the same founders, the same architecture reviews and the same launch discipline. Nothing is handed to a team you have never met.

06 Process

A short route
from brief to production.

Four stages, no relay race. The founders stay on the project from the first sketch to the release and beyond.

  1. 01

    Deep strategy

    We dissect the product, the audience and the constraint that matters. The output is a route, not a wish list.

    1–2 weeks
  2. 02

    Blueprint & architecture

    Clickable screens, a design system and a system architecture you can interrogate before a line of code is written.

    2–3 weeks
  3. 03

    Engineering sprints

    Two-week cycles with working builds on your phone and a live staging environment. You review the product, not a status deck.

    Continuous
  4. 04

    Launch & altitude

    Store deployment, monitoring and measured iteration. We stay responsible after the invoice — as we do on our own products.

    Beyond launch
